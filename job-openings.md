## Prospective Students and Researchers

Please do not send unsolicited or generic applications by email. Due to the volume of inquiries, such messages, including automatically generated or AI-assisted application emails, are generally not reviewed.

If you are interested in joining the lab, there are two appropriate routes:

1. **Come with a concrete funding plan.** Contact me if you have your own funding or have identified a specific fellowship, scholarship, or project call for which you are realistically eligible. Your message should identify the funding scheme, deadline, and a concrete research idea.
2. **Talk to me in person.** If you do not yet have a concrete funding opportunity or proposal, the best way to start a conversation is to meet me at a conference, workshop, or other scientific event.

Positions funded directly by my group will be advertised separately through an open call or filled through targeted recruitment. Sending a generic application email does not increase your chances of being considered.
