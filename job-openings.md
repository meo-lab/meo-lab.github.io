  <div class="job-posting__letter">

    <h2 style="color:#163f74; font-size:1.35rem; margin: 0 0 0.5rem;">Student Assistant (SHK) – Neural Earth Fields Hackathon</h2>
    <p style="color:#2d5f9e; font-size:0.88rem; font-weight:700; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:0; margin-bottom:1.2rem;">University of Bonn &nbsp;·&nbsp; Start: as soon as possible &nbsp;·&nbsp; Until December 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; 8–20 h/week</p>

    <p>We are looking for a Bachelor's or Master's student at the University of Bonn to support the technical preparation and organization of the <strong>Neural Earth Fields Hackathon</strong>, taking place <strong>7–8 December 2026 in Bonn</strong>.</p>

    <p>The hackathon focuses on machine learning, geospatial representation learning, and learning functions and representations over the Earth. You will work with us in the months leading up to the event and help support the hackathon itself on-site.</p>

    <p>The position combines technical work with event organization — we are looking for people who are excited about one or both of these aspects.</p>

    <h3 style="color:#163f74; font-size:1.05rem; margin: 1.4rem 0 0.5rem;">Main tasks</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Helping develop the hackathon challenge website</li>
      <li>Preparing and structuring benchmark datasets</li>
      <li>Developing starter code and example notebooks for participants</li>
      <li>Creating an introductory notebook/tutorial for working with geospatial functions and spherical geometry on the Earth</li>
      <li>Testing benchmarks, datasets, code, and participant instructions</li>
      <li>Supporting general hackathon preparation and organization</li>
      <li>Helping with on-site event coordination on 7–8 December</li>
      <li>Other small organizational and technical tasks related to preparing the event</li>
    </ul>

    <h3 style="color:#163f74; font-size:1.05rem; margin: 1.4rem 0 0.5rem;">Who we are looking for</h3>
    <p>The position is open to Bachelor's and Master's students enrolled at the University of Bonn. Experience in one or more of the following areas is advantageous, but not required:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Computer science / programming</li>
      <li>Python</li>
      <li>Machine learning</li>
      <li>Geospatial data / GIS / Earth observation</li>
      <li>Git / GitHub / Jupyter notebooks</li>
      <li>Web development</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We are equally interested in students who enjoy organizing events and working with people. Previous experience organizing student activities, workshops, hackathons, or events — for example through a Fachschaft or another student organization — is very welcome.</p>
    <p>You do not need to already know everything listed above. <strong>Curiosity, reliability, willingness to learn, and enthusiasm for helping build a great hackathon are more important.</strong></p>

    <h3 style="color:#163f74; font-size:1.05rem; margin: 1.4rem 0 0.5rem;">Position details</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Start:</strong> as soon as possible</li>
      <li><strong>Duration:</strong> until December 2026</li>
      <li><strong>Workload:</strong> approximately 8–20 hours per week</li>
      <li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> Bachelor's or Master's students enrolled at the University of Bonn</li>
      <li><strong>Location:</strong> University of Bonn / Bonn, with on-site participation required for the hackathon on 7–8 December 2026</li>
    </ul>

    <h3 style="color:#163f74; font-size:1.05rem; margin: 1.4rem 0 0.5rem;">How to apply</h3>
    <p>Applications are informal and can be sent by email to <a href="mailto:marc.russwurm@uni-bonn.de">marc.russwurm@uni-bonn.de</a> with the subject line <strong>SHK – Neural Earth Fields Hackathon</strong>.</p>
    <p>Please include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>A short introduction</li>
      <li>Your current Bachelor's/Master's program and semester</li>
      <li>A brief description of relevant technical and/or organizational experience</li>
      <li>Your approximate availability between now and December</li>
      <li>Optionally: a GitHub profile or other examples of previous work</li>
    </ul>
    <p>We look forward to hearing from you!</p>
