title: "Neural Earth Fields Benchmark & Hackathon in Bonn"
date: 2026-10-15
---

We are organising the **[Neural Earth Fields Benchmark & Hackathon](https://neuralearthfields.github.io)**, a 24-hour sprint on **7–8 December 2026** at the IMPULSE House of the University of Bonn. Teams will build neural networks that take longitude and latitude as input and reconstruct global geospatial layers, from water storage anomalies and ESA WorldCover land cover to iNaturalist biodiversity observations. The event is co-organised by Marc Rußwurm, Jürgen Gall, and Jürgen Kusche (University of Bonn) with Konstantin Klemmer (UCL), Alistair Francis (Asterisk Labs), Isaac Corley (Taylor Geospatial), and Kyle Gao (Aalto University).

Early-career researchers in geospatial science or machine learning can apply until **16 October 2026**. [More information →](https://neuralearthfields.github.io)
