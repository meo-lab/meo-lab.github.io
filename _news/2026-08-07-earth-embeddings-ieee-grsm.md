Our perspective paper **"[Earth Embeddings: Toward AI-centric Representations of our Planet](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11636001)"** has been published in the **IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Magazine**. We argue that Earth Embeddings represent the next abstraction layer for geospatial AI — shared representations of our planet learned from satellite imagery, maps, elevation, climate, and other geospatial data.
